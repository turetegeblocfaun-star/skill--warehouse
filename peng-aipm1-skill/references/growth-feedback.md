## 1. Purpose and trigger

Help the beginner build transferable AI product judgment, not only finish one document.

Add a growth summary after a phase artifact, formal review, professional document, learning document, launch plan, or post-launch review. On ordinary turns, mention growth only when a specific reasoning pattern creates an immediate learning opportunity.

Do not interrupt every brainstorm with a scorecard.

## 2. Attribution rules

Separate three sources of difficulty:

- **Project gap:** The project lacks evidence, data, owner, access, or a decision.
- **Coach/AI gap:** The Skill misunderstood, asked poorly, overreached, or failed to explain.
- **Learner capability gap:** The user's reasoning reveals a repeatable skill that needs development.

Never label a missing project fact as a personal weakness. Never praise without evidence. Never turn one answer into a permanent trait.

Use the user's decision process, corrections, tradeoff reasoning, and ability to explain evidence as evidence. Let later behavior revise earlier judgments.

## 3. Capability dimensions

Track only dimensions demonstrated by the project:

### Product management

- Opportunity and problem discovery
- Stakeholder, user, scenario, and need insight
- Evidence, research, and assumption management
- Product value, strategy, market, and business judgment
- Outcome, metric, and experiment design
- MVP, priority, non-goal, and tradeoff judgment
- Journey, interaction, requirement, and acceptance quality
- Delivery, collaboration, launch, and iteration

### AI product management

- AI suitability and deterministic-baseline judgment
- Human-AI mental models, trust, feedback, and control
- Prompt, RAG, fine-tune, agent, hybrid, and model tradeoffs
- Data, permissions, provenance, and knowledge governance
- Golden set, quality rubric, evaluation, and regression design
- Safety, risk tier, guardrails, human review, and incident thinking
- Agent autonomy, tool permission, confirmation, and recovery
- Cost, latency, observability, rollout, and lifecycle judgment

## 4. Evidence levels

Avoid fake precision and numerical ratings. Use:

- `尚未体现`
- `正在建立`
- `能在引导下完成`
- `可以独立完成`
- `可以判断并挑战他人方案`

Attach a project-specific evidence sentence to every level statement.

## 5. Milestone feedback format

```markdown
### AI 产品经理成长总结

本阶段已表现出的能力
- {{能力 + 具体证据}}

最优先提升的方面
- {{最多 1–3 项；说明本项目证据与影响}}

下一步练习
- {{与当前项目直接相关的小练习}}

需要区分
- 项目缺口：{{如有}}
- 引导缺口：{{如有}}
- 个人能力：{{如有}}
```

Do not force all fields when there is no evidence.

Useful exercises include rewriting a problem statement without the solution, building five golden cases, comparing a deterministic baseline with RAG, adding acceptance conditions, defining a human confirmation point, or mapping cost per successful outcome.

## 6. Final growth report

End the learning document with:

- Initial reasoning pattern
- Material viewpoint changes and why
- Capabilities demonstrated with evidence
- Capabilities still requiring guided practice
- Three highest-value next exercises
- A suggested next project or responsibility level

Keep the report respectful, falsifiable, and specific. The learner owns the final interpretation.
